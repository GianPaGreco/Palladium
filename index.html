Palladium (Pd)

Atomic Number: 46 | Transition Metal

Basic Information

  • Name: Palladium
  • Chemical Symbol: Pd
  • Atomic Mass: 106.42 u
  • Atomic Number: 46
  • Classification: Metal (Transition Metal)

Physical Properties

  • Density: 12.02 g/cm³
  • Melting Point: 1554°C
  • Boiling Point: 2963°C
  • State at Room Temperature: Solid
  • Appearance: Silvery-white metal

Chemical Properties

Palladium is resistant to corrosion and oxidation in air. It forms compounds with hydrogen and is an excellent catalyst for chemical reactions, especially hydrogenation.

Location on Periodic Table

  • Group: 10
  • Period: 5

Bohr Model

  • Protons: 46
  • Electrons: 46
  • Neutrons: ~60

Uses in the World

  • Catalytic converters in automobiles
  • Electronics and jewelry
  • Dental materials
  • Hydrogen storage and purification

Discovery

Palladium was discovered by William Hyde Wollaston in 1803. It was named after the asteroid Pallas.

Interesting Fact & Slogan

Palladium is rarer than gold and is essential in green technologies. Slogan: "Palladium – Powering Clean Energy and Innovation!"
